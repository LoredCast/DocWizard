# Springer LNCS

Springer's Lecture Notes in Computer Science proceedings format (the `llncs` class), as its guidelines for
authors describe it.

- **Page**: A4 with the LNCS text block of 12.2 × 19.3 cm, centred. 10 pt New Computer Modern, the font of
  the LaTeX class, single-spaced and justified; paragraphs indented 15 pt.
- **Title block**: title in bold, authors, institutes and e-mail addresses, then a small abstract led by
  "Abstract." and the `keywords`.
- **Headings**: "1 Introduction" and "1.1 Background" in bold; level 3 in bold and level 4 in italics, run
  into their paragraph.
- **Captions**: "Fig. 1." below figures and "Table 1." above tables, labels in bold, in a smaller size.
- **References**: numbered "[1]" in Springer's LNCS style (`springer-lecture-notes-in-computer-science`).

Springer asks for the source files of the camera-ready version; export LaTeX or Word for that.
