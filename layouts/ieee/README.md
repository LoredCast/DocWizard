# IEEE paper

Follows the *IEEE Editorial Style Manual for Authors* (2024) for headings, captions, references and dates, and
the geometry of IEEE's two-column conference and transactions template.

- **Page**: US Letter in two columns, 10 pt Tinos (Times metrics), single-spaced and justified.
- **Title block**: title, author, affiliation and e-mail centred across both columns. The abstract follows as
  one paragraph led by a bold italic *Abstract*— and set in bold, then the bold "Index Terms—" line from the
  `keywords` in the front matter.
- **Headings**: level 1 "I. Introduction" numbered with Roman numerals, centred, in small caps; level 2
  "A. Formal Frameworks" flush left in italics; level 3 "1) Title:" indented and run into its paragraph,
  in italics, ending with a colon; level 4 "1a) Title:" likewise, indented further.
- **Figures**: "Fig. 1." and the caption below, in a smaller size.
- **Tables**: "TABLE I" centred above the table with the title on the next line in small caps.
- **Footnotes**: at the foot of the column, in a smaller size.
- **Dates**: day month year, "4 June 2002", when given as `2002-06-04`.
- **References**: under "References" in small caps, numbered "[1]" in order of citation.

**Citations.** IEEE (the CSL style `ieee`): numbered citations in square brackets, "[1, p. 12]".

Small caps are drawn as reduced capitals, since the bundled fonts have no small-caps glyphs. The abstract spans
both columns here; in IEEE's own template it sits in the first column.
