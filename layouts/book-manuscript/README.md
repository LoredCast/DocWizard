# Book manuscript

A nonfiction book manuscript for submission to a publisher, after the University of Chicago Press's
manuscript preparation guidelines and *The Chicago Manual of Style* (chapter 2), which most English-language
academic publishers follow.

- **Page**: US Letter, one-inch margins, page numbers top right.
- **Text**: Tinos at 12 pt, double-spaced, ragged right, not hyphenated, half-inch paragraph indents; notes in
  the size of the text.
- **Headings**: unnumbered, bold, in three sizes (18, 16 and 14 pt for level 1 to 3) so the levels are easy to
  tell apart; each chapter (level 1) starts on a new page.
- **Figures and tables**: numbered by chapter, "Figure 2.3", "Table 1.1"; cross-references (`@fig:map`)
  follow.
- **Title page**: title, subtitle and "by" a third of the way down, the draft and date at the foot.
- **Bibliography**: on a new page.

**Citations.** Chicago notes and bibliography (18th edition); author-date and the other bundled styles can be
chosen, in which case the list is headed "References".
