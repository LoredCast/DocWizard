# Thesis

A general layout for bachelor's, master's and doctoral theses, following what most university guidelines
ask for. Your department's own rules win: change the margins, citation style or title page in the layout
editor (Layouts → Customise) and keep everything else.

- **Page**: A4, 12 pt Libertinus Serif, 1.5 line spacing, justified. A 3 cm left margin for binding, 2.5 cm
  elsewhere. The running header repeats the title above a rule.
- **Front matter**: a title page with `institution`, `faculty`, `department`, the kind of thesis
  (`paper-type`, e.g. "Master's thesis"), `submission` ("submitted for the degree of …"), `program`, the
  author and `student-id`, `supervisor`, and place and date at the foot. Then the abstract on its own page, a
  declaration of authorship to sign, and the table of contents with lists of figures and tables. These pages
  are numbered i, ii, iii; the text starts again at 1.
- **Chapters**: level 1 headings ("1 Introduction") start on a new page, a little lower down; sections are
  numbered 1.1 and 1.1.1. Figures and tables are numbered by chapter ("Figure 2.3"), with a bold label.
- **Bibliography**: on a new page under "Bibliography". Options: Chicago author-date (default), APA, Harvard,
  IEEE, Chicago notes or DIN 1505-2; line spacing and font size.

Write the declaration's wording in `declaration.text` in the layout editor if your university prescribes it.
