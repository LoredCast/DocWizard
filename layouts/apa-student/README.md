# APA student paper

Follows the *Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association* (7th edition, 2020), chapter 2,
and APA's Student Paper Setup Guide and sample student paper.

- **Page** (2.22, 2.18): US Letter with one-inch margins. The page number sits top right on every page,
  the title page included. Student papers have no running head.
- **Text** (2.19, 2.21, 2.23, 2.24): Tinos at 12 pt (Times New Roman metrics), double-spaced throughout with
  no extra space between paragraphs, ragged right, half-inch indents.
- **Title page** (2.3): the title three to four lines down, bold and centred, the subtitle on the next line
  (it follows a colon). After a blank double-spaced line: author, affiliation, course, instructor and due date,
  each on its own line. Give the affiliation as the author's `affiliation`, in the form "Department of
  Psychology, University of …".
- **Abstract** (2.9): when the paper has one, it goes on page 2 under a centred, bold "Abstract", without a
  first-line indent. Keywords follow on an indented line starting with italic *Keywords:*.
- **Title on the first page of text** (2.11): repeated bold and centred above the introduction.
- **Headings** (2.27, table 2.3): level 1 centred bold; level 2 flush left bold; level 3 flush left bold italic;
  level 4 indented bold, ending with a period, with the text following on the same line. Level 5 is set like
  level 4 (APA's level 5 adds italics, which the layout does not distinguish).
- **Block quotations** (8.27): indented half an inch, double-spaced.
- **Tables and figures** (7.10, 7.22): the number in bold ("Table 1", "Figure 1") above the table or figure,
  flush left, and the title in italics on the next line.
- **References** (2.12, 9.43): on a new page under a centred, bold "References", double-spaced with a
  half-inch hanging indent.

**Citations.** APA 7th edition (the CSL style `apa`).
