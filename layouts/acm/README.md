# ACM conference paper

The geometry and typography of ACM's `acmart` class in its `sigconf` format, used by most ACM conferences.
Submissions to ACM still go through their LaTeX or Word templates; this layout is for drafting, sharing and
reviewing a paper that looks like the final one.

- **Page**: US Letter, two columns 0.33 in apart, 0.75 in side margins. 9 pt Libertinus Serif, the
  continuation of the Linux Libertine family that acmart uses.
- **Title block**: title flush left in bold, then authors, affiliations and e-mail, the abstract under
  "Abstract", and the `keywords` from the front matter.
- **Headings**: "1 INTRODUCTION" bold in capitals, "1.1 Background" bold, level 3 and 4 italic and run into
  their paragraph.
- **Captions**: "Figure 1:" and "Table 1:" with bold labels; tables are captioned above.
- **References**: numbered, in the ACM reference format (the CSL style `association-for-computing-machinery`),
  under "References" in a smaller size.

The CCS concepts block and the rights statement in the first column's footer are not set.
