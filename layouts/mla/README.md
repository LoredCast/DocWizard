# MLA paper

Follows the MLA's formatting guidance for research papers (the MLA Style Center's "Formatting a Research
Paper" and its sample papers) and the *MLA Handbook*'s citation style.

- **Page**: US Letter with one-inch margins. Every page carries your surname and the page number in
  the top right corner, half an inch from the edge ("Doe 1"). There is no title page.
- **Text**: Tinos at 12 pt (Times New Roman metrics), double-spaced throughout, ragged right and not
  hyphenated. Paragraphs are indented half an inch, the first one too.
- **Heading block**: your name, the instructor, the course and the date, flush left and double-spaced
  at the top of the first page (`author`, `instructor`, `course` and `date` in the front matter). The date
  is written day month year ("13 November 2026") when you give it as `2026-11-13`.
- **Title**: centred on the next line, in the same type as the text, with no bold or italics. A subtitle
  follows after a colon.
- **Headings**: MLA prescribes none. The layout sets level 1 in bold, level 2 in italics and lower levels
  plain, all flush left and in the size of the text.
- **Block quotations**: indented half an inch, double-spaced, without quotation marks.
- **Notes**: footnotes in the size of the text, double-spaced and indented half an inch.
- **Tables and illustrations**: "Table 1" with its title flush left above the table; "Fig. 1." and the
  caption below a figure.
- **Works Cited**: on a new page under a centred, plain "Works Cited", double-spaced with a half-inch
  hanging indent. In other languages the heading is translated.

**Citations.** MLA 9th edition (the CSL style `modern-language-association`): parenthetical author and page,
"(Smith and Lee 12)".
