# Turabian student paper

Follows Kate L. Turabian, *A Manual for Writers of Research Papers, Theses, and Dissertations*, 9th edition
(2018), appendix A, and the Chicago "Turabian" student tip sheets.

- **Page** (A.1.1, A.1.4): US Letter with one-inch margins. Page numbers sit top right, half an inch from the
  edge. The title page counts as page 1 but shows no number, so the text starts on page 2.
- **Text** (A.1.2, A.1.3): Tinos at 12 pt, which has the metrics of Times New Roman. Double-spaced, ragged
  right and not hyphenated, with half-inch paragraph indents.
- **Title page** (A.2.1.2): the title begins a third of the way down. It is bold, centred and slightly larger
  than the text. A subtitle goes on its own line after a colon. Your name, the course and the date follow
  lower down, double-spaced (`author`, `course`, `instructor` and `date` in the front matter).
- **Subheads** (A.2.2.4): the first level is bold and centred, the second regular and centred, the third bold
  and flush left. All are the size of the text, with more space before them than after, and they stay with
  the next paragraph.
- **Block quotations** (25.2.2): single-spaced and indented half an inch, with a blank line before and after.
- **Footnotes** (16.3.4): single-spaced with a blank line between notes. They are slightly smaller than the
  text, with the first line indented half an inch, a number followed by a full stop, and a short rule above.
- **Tables and figures** (26.2.2, 26.3.2): "Table 1." titles go flush left above the table, and "Figure 1."
  captions flush left below the figure. Both are single-spaced.
- **Bibliography** (A.2.3.5): on a new page under a centred "Bibliography". Entries are single-spaced with a
  blank line between them and a half-inch hanging indent. With the author-date style the heading becomes
  "Reference List" (chapter 18).
- **Contents**, when turned on, is headed "Contents" (A.2.1.7).

**Citations.** Turabian's 9th edition follows the 17th edition of *The Chicago Manual of Style*, so the
default is Chicago 17th-edition notes and bibliography. The first note for a source is in full, including the
place of publication. Later notes use the author and short title (16.4.1) and no *ibid.* Two other 17th-edition
choices are offered: shortened notes throughout, and author-date. The Chicago 18th-edition styles (2024) are
also available, for instructors who ask for the current *Manual*.

Short notes use the main title without its subtitle. With BibTeX that works on its own. In CSL-JSON, use the
entry's `title-short` (Zotero calls it "Short Title"); when it is missing, DocWizard uses the part of the
title before the colon.

Options: line spacing, title page and the citation style.
