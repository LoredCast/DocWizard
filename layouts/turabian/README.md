# Turabian student paper

After the Turabian student tip sheets (*A Manual for Writers*, 9th edition): US Letter with one-inch
margins, Tinos at 12 pt (the metrics of Times New Roman), double-spaced and ragged right, half-inch
paragraph indents, page numbers top right, and a title page with the bold title about a third of the way
down and your name, the course and the date below. The title page counts as page 1 but shows no number.

Subheads are bold and centred (first level), regular and centred (second) and bold flush left (third).
Block quotations, notes and bibliography entries are single-spaced with a blank line between them; the
bibliography starts on a new page under the heading "Bibliography", with half-inch hanging indents.

Citations default to Chicago shortened notes and bibliography; switch to full notes or to author-date
(then name the list "Reference List" in the layout editor or with `reference-section-title:`).

Front matter it uses: `title`, `subtitle`, `author`, `course`, `instructor`, `date`.

Options: line spacing, title page and the citation style.
