# University of Oxford

After the *University of Oxford Style Guide 2026*: the University's recommended font Noto Serif, British
English (`en-GB`, so quotation marks are single, with double ones inside), ragged-right text, footnotes in a
smaller size under a rule, and citations in the Oxford Guide to Style (notes and bibliography). Titles are
left-aligned with the author, affiliation, department, course and date beneath.

The guide is about writing more than page layout. What a layout can't do for you: dates written as
"13 April", "p21" and "pp7–8" for page references, no full stops in "eg", "ie" and "etc", and titles of
whole works in italics, of parts in single quotation marks. The citation style takes care of the last one
in references.

Front matter it uses: `title`, `subtitle`, `author` (with `affiliation`), `department`, `course`, `date`,
`abstract`.

Options: paper size, font size, line spacing, section numbering, title page, table of contents and the
citation style.
