# Novel manuscript

William Shunn's *Proper Manuscript Format* for novels (shunn.net). It builds on the
[short story manuscript](../manuscript-story/README.md) and changes the opening:

- **Title page**: its own page with your contact block in the top left, and the title, "by" and your name
  and the rounded word count centred. It is not numbered; the first page of text is page 1.
- **Header**: "Surname / Title / 1" on every page of text, chapter openings included.
- **Chapters**: each level 1 heading ("Chapter 1" or the chapter's title) starts a new page, about a third of the
  way down.

Everything else, the text, scene breaks and word count, is as in the short story layout.
