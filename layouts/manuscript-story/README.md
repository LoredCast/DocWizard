# Short story manuscript

William Shunn's *Proper Manuscript Format* for short stories (shunn.net, the form most fiction magazines ask for).

- **Page**: US Letter, one-inch margins. From page 2 the top right corner reads "Surname / Title / 2"
  (the `short-title` from the front matter, or the title).
- **Text**: Tinos at 12 pt (Times New Roman metrics), double-spaced, ragged right, not hyphenated, half-inch
  indents for every paragraph. Italics stay italics.
- **First page**: your name, address, phone and e-mail single-spaced in the top left corner (`author`,
  `address`, `phone`, `email`, `contact`), the word count in the top right, rounded to the nearest hundred
  ("about 4,300 words"; to the nearest 500 above 17,500). The title is centred about a third of the way down,
  "by" and your name a double space below, and the story starts two double spaces after that. The count is
  made from the text; set `word-count:` yourself to override it.
- **Scene breaks**: `***` in the text becomes a centred "#".
- **Headings**: plain and centred, if you use them.

In another document language the word count and "by" are translated ("ca. 4.300 Wörter", "von").
