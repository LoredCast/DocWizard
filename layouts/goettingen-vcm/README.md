# Göttingen seminar paper (Value Chain Management)

Follows the guidelines for seminar papers and theses of the Chair of Value Chain Management (Prof. Matthias
Schulz), Faculty of Business and Economics, University of Göttingen (version 01/2024).

- **Page**: A4 with 2.5 cm margins all round, page numbers centred in the footer.
- **Text**: Tinos at 12 pt (Times New Roman metrics), double-spaced, justified and hyphenated, with indented
  paragraphs. Footnotes, which the guide asks you to avoid, are smaller and single-spaced.
- **Title page**: unnumbered. The submission note (`submission`, e.g. "Seminar paper presented in the winter
  term 2026/27 as part of the examination in …"), the submission date, and at the foot your name, place of
  birth, matriculation number, address, e-mail, phone, semester and supervisor (`author`, `birthplace`,
  `student-id`, `address`, `email`, `phone`, `semester`, `supervisor`). Put the university and chair in
  `subtitle` or `submission` as your instructor asks.
- **Front matter**: contents, list of figures and list of tables on their own page, numbered with Roman
  numerals (I, II, …).
- **Text**: numbered decimally (1, 1.1, 1.1.1) and paginated in Arabic numerals from 1.
- **References and appendix**: on a new page, continuing the Roman numbers of the front matter.

**Citations.** APA 7th edition (the CSL style `apa`), as the guide requires.
